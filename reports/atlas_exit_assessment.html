Recommended Priority Fixes

The five highest-priority actions for the next 90 days, ranked by deal impact. For the complete domain-by-domain remediation plan and cost estimates, see the Value Recovery Roadmap above.

Fix 1HC
Document Key-Person Dependencies and Retention Agreements
This fix directly addresses Human Capital Risk (0.0/10 — CRITICAL RISK). Create a written organizational chart identifying all revenue-critical roles, their current compensation, and any existing retention or non-compete agreements; simultaneously draft and execute retention letters for top 3–5 revenue producers with 12–24 month post-close stay bonuses. A buyer's diligence team will immediately assess key-person risk and post-close operational continuity—absence of this documentation will trigger a material valuation haircut or deal blockage, so documented succession readiness and locked retention is essential to preserve the 1.5–1.8× SDE multiple.
Fix 2OR
Systematize Owner-Dependent Revenue and Decision Authority
This fix directly addresses Owner Risk (2.8/10 — CRITICAL RISK). Map all owner-dependent revenue relationships, customer contracts, and key decisions; transfer at least 40% of customer-facing authority to a documented COO or Operations Lead with clear decision-making authority and written delegation of signing rights. Buyers will require evidence that the business can operate without founder presence—lack of this documented transition plan will force extended seller involvement, escrow holds, and earn-out structures that destroy deal certainty and valuation.
Fix 3DR
Produce Audited Financial Statements and Revenue Attribution
This fix directly addresses Diligence Risk (3.6/10 — NEEDS WORK). Engage a third-party accountant to produce 2–3 years of reviewed or compiled financial statements with clear revenue attribution by customer segment, channel, and service line; resolve all accrual vs. cash timing discrepancies and produce a reconciliation memo. Buyers will scrutinize financial documentation during initial due diligence—weak or unclear records will extend timeline, increase buyer skepticism, and justify a working capital adjustment or valuation discount, so clean, credible financials are non-negotiable to close at target multiple.
Fix 4TM
Complete Cybersecurity Assessment and Remediation Roadmap
Technology &amp; Systems Maturity scores 3.2/10 (CRITICAL RISK) and carries 7% weight; conduct a third-party cybersecurity gap assessment covering data protection, customer information security, and incident response readiness, then produce a detailed remediation roadmap with timeline and cost estimates. Cybersecurity failures and data breach liability are material deal-killers for security-focused buyers—a credible third-party assessment and documented remediation plan will reduce buyer anxiety, protect valuation, and accelerate buyer confidence during tech diligence.
Fix 5CQ
Develop Customer Concentration Risk Mitigation and Expansion Plan
Customer Quality scores 4.8/10 (NEEDS WORK) and carries 20% weight; document the top 10 customer relationships, renewal history, and contractual terms; identify and execute signed expansion agreements or new customer pilots with 2–3 mid-market prospects to demonstrate diversification and growth momentum post-acquisition. Buyer diligence will stress-test revenue stability and growth trajectory—concentration risk and weak customer stickiness will compress valuation multiples, so proof of diversification and pipeline growth strengthens pricing and buyer confidence.
